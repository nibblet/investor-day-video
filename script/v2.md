  Grok team working in the background: a morning brief, a tray of work that

  the agents prepare, Paul approves. The Chief of Staff drafts a notice and does

  the suggested-task lane, and notes, 2026-09-24 → 10-08). Nothing is invented;
  names, addresses and dollar amounts on live deals are stripped.
- **New cold open at 6:40 AM**, truck in the dark, light bar waking.
- **Valley Station stays the deal on screen** (sold, numbers approved). Today's
  live walkthrough stays out: that negotiation is still open.
- Runtime goes from ~75 s to **~88 s** (still inside a Reel). Spoken words: **164**
  (v1 had 179). Fewer words, more room: the agent cards need a beat of silence
  to read.

## The produced cut (same script, different finish)

The static cut stays as the safe LinkedIn version. The produced cut is for
Reels and TikTok:

- **Cold open hits on the light bar.** Black → light bar wakes on a low synth
  hit → brief cards drop in one per beat of the music.
- **Screens float, not composite.** Phones tilt into frame in 3D beside the
  footage, catching the light; cards fly out of the phone into the frame around
  you.
- **The background tray stays alive.** During the walkthrough, small agent
  cards tick from working → done in the corner, with a soft tick sound each time.
- **Scene 6 is the money shot.** One message bubble, then five cards flip in
  sequence, 6 frames apart, each with a click.
- **Kinetic captions** 2–3 words at a time, synced to the VO, kept above the
  bottom fifth.
- **Music:** steady, warm, slightly electronic pulse (not trailer, not EDM).
  Cut points on the downbeat; duck under VO.
- **Ending:** cards collapse into one tile, *9 handled · 1 needed you*, then
  the truck locks and the light bar dims. Hold black 1 s, then the question.
